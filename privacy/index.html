2. The short version

  • Our games do not require a Gillund Labs account. Current game builds do not send gameplay statistics or saved progress to Gillund Labs or an analytics company.
  • Settings, progress, trial timers, designs and purchase-related records are handled on your device. There is no Gillund Labs cloud-save service.
  • We receive information you choose to send in feedback or email. Website hosting and feedback services also handle the connection information needed to deliver their services.
  • We do not show ads in our games or on our website. We do not sell or rent personal information, or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, including information about minors. Necessary disclosures to hosting, feedback and payment services are explained below.
  • Mobile purchases, where available, use Apple’s App Store or Google Play. Our website and browser demos do not take payments.
  • We plan to add privacy-conscious gameplay analytics. That feature is not enabled in the reviewed builds. Section 6 is a proposed description, not a statement that collection has already started.

Gameplay and device information kept locally

Depending on the game and version, your browser or app stores settings, theme and sound preferences, progress, scores, unlocked content, coins, trial start and elapsed-play times, and purchase entitlement or transaction records. Vacuum Maze and Fantastic Apparatus also save custom levels or designs locally. These games can produce text share codes; creating or copying a code does not upload it to Gillund Labs. Vacuum Maze uses device motion and orientation to steer the game. Sensor readings are processed in memory on your device; the game does not save a motion history or send the readings to us. Browser or operating-system permission behavior varies. You can use manual controls if motion access is unavailable or declined. The game can also provide haptic feedback and keep the screen awake during play.

Maxwell’s Demon reads screen size and display density, and, on supported iPhones, the device model, to scale its physics to the screen. Optional screen calibration is saved locally. It does not read a payment card when calibrating the screen. None of the reviewed games requests access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise location for gameplay, or reads an advertising identifier.

Your operating system or device backup service may copy app data according to your own settings. That is separate from a Gillund Labs cloud-save service.

Maxwell’s Demon local statistics

The current statistics setting is labeled “Share anonymous gameplay stats” and is on by default. Despite that label, the reviewed release implementation has no network analytics provider or collection endpoint and sends nothing off the device.

When enabled, the module creates a random installation identifier and a local queue of events containing event names, timestamps, app version, platform, game modes, settings, round results, achievements and screen-scale categories. It does not include your name, email, precise location, advertising ID or a hardware identifier. The queue is capped at 300 events. Its current no-provider adapter discards records locally in batches of up to 50 during periodic processing, normally every 30 seconds while online and on certain app visibility or connectivity changes. Offline or unprocessed records can remain locally. The random identifier has no automatic expiry.

Turning this setting off stops new local events and deletes the local queue and random identifier. A random identifier is not a guarantee of legal anonymity: it would generally be pseudonymous data if later transmitted. Vacuum Maze and Fantastic Apparatus have no corresponding online gameplay analytics implementation in the reviewed code.

4. Why we use information and our legal bases

We use only the information needed for the purposes described here: delivering the website and games, remembering your choices and progress, providing trials and purchased content, restoring eligible purchases, responding to feedback, fixing problems, preventing abuse and meeting legal obligations.

Where the EU or UK GDPR applies, the relevant legal bases are:

  • Contract or steps you request before a contract: processing necessary to supply the game functionality or purchase support you request.
  • Legitimate interests: answering voluntary correspondence, investigating bugs, maintaining security and preventing abuse, where those interests are not overridden by your rights. This is not a blanket basis for optional tracking.
  • Legal obligations: complying with applicable recordkeeping, consumer-rights, privacy or other legal requirements.
  • Consent: optional processing or device storage where the law requires consent, including any future analytics where applicable. Merely reading this policy, playing a game or submitting ordinary feedback is not blanket consent to unrelated uses. You may withdraw consent without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

Local game choices and purchase checks do not amount to profiling for decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. We do not use personal information to make such decisions about you.

7. Cookies, local storage and offline caching

Our reviewed site and game code does not set advertising or analytics cookies. That does not mean no device storage is used:

  • Browser localStorage holds game saves, preferences, trial records, custom levels, local purchase records and the Maxwell’s Demon local statistics described above.
  • Native preferences also store Maxwell’s Demon trial and credited-transaction records through Capacitor Preferences (UserDefaults on iOS and SharedPreferences on Android).
  • Service workers and Cache Storage, when running a multi-file browser/PWA build, cache game files for offline loading and refresh them from the same site. The reviewed single-file website demos and native apps skip service-worker registration. No gameplay database using IndexedDB was found in the reviewed application code.

These mechanisms are not used for cross-site advertising. Your browser can block or clear site data; doing so can erase saves, custom designs, trial records or local coin balances. Some in-game “Erase progress” controls deliberately keep purchase records, trial state or custom levels. Removing all app/site data may therefore require browser or operating-system controls. Restore Purchases can restore eligible store entitlements, but does not guarantee restoration of spent consumables, local progress or designs. Device backup copies may persist according to your backup settings.

We do not currently interpret browser Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signals in the application code. Because we do not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising or run targeted advertising, those activities are off for everyone regardless of such signals. Any change would require a new review and legally required controls. Provider websites have their own cookie practices.

10. Your choices and privacy rights

You can play without creating a Gillund Labs account or sending feedback, control local game settings, turn off Maxwell’s Demon’s local statistics, clear device data and manage platform permissions. We cannot remotely retrieve or delete a save that exists only on your device. Contact us if you need help finding the appropriate control.

Depending on where you live and whether the relevant law applies, you may have the right to access or obtain a portable copy of personal information, correct it, request deletion, restrict processing, object to processing based on legitimate interests, or withdraw consent. Exceptions may apply, for example where records must be kept by law or to protect others’ rights.

EU / EEA and United Kingdom

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, you have the rights above, subject to their conditions, and may complain to your local data-protection authority. UK residents can contact the Information Commissioner’s Office; EEA residents can find their authority through the European Data Protection Board. You do not need to contact us first to complain.

California

Where the CCPA, as amended by the CPRA, applies, California residents can request the categories and specific pieces of information collected, sources, purposes and recipient categories; access and portability; correction; deletion; and freedom from unlawful discrimination. They can opt out of sale or sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising and, where applicable, limit certain uses of sensitive personal information. We do not engage in those sale, advertising-sharing or sensitive-data profiling activities. An authorized agent may submit a request with the authority required by law. CCPA coverage depends on statutory thresholds and other conditions; this notice does not assume every small studio meets them.

Texas

Where the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act (TDPSA) applies, Texas consumers can confirm processing, access, correct, delete and obtain a portable copy of their data, and opt out of targeted advertising, data sales and certain consequential profiling. We do not engage in those opt-out activities. The TDPSA generally exempts qualifying small businesses, subject to its rule on sales of sensitive data; we do not sell sensitive data. We will not unlawfully discriminate against you for exercising privacy rights.

How to make a request or appeal

Email the monitored privacy address in section 1, or use the website feedback form with “Privacy request” in the message and a reply address. State what you want us to do and enough context to locate the relevant correspondence or record. No new account is required. Do not send a password, full payment-card number or government ID. We may request proportionate verification for access, correction or deletion, and proof of an agent’s authority where appropriate. We use verification information only to process and secure the request.

Where applicable law requires, we respond without undue delay, normally within one month for GDPR requests or 45 days for CCPA/TDPSA access, correction or deletion requests, with any lawful extension explained within the initial response period. Applicable shorter deadlines take precedence. Requests are generally free, subject to legally permitted exceptions. We will explain a denial and any rights to challenge it.

To appeal a denied Texas request, reply to the privacy address with “Privacy appeal” and the original request details. Where TDPSA applies, we will provide the appeal decision and reasons within 60 days. If the appeal is denied, you can file a consumer complaint with the Texas Attorney General. You can also ask us to reconsider another privacy response or contact your competent regulator. We consider reasonable privacy requests even where a particular statute does not apply.
