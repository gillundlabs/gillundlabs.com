About Gillund’s Laboratory

Gillund Labs is a small independent studio making beautiful games about real physics. Our games share one look: a polished brass instrument on a dark wood bench, with a scientist’s notebook kept alongside.

We try to be honest. Every factual notebook page names a source, every analogy is labelled as one, and every place a game bends the rules says so.

There are no ads and no trackers on this site. The browser versions of our games are free to try; the full games are coming to the App Store, Google Play and Steam.
