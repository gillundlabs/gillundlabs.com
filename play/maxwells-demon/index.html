Maxwell's Demon

An apparatus for the trapping of single atoms, after J. C. Maxwell (1867)

Chambers in microgravity (orbital laboratory)

The atom isn't a billiard ball. It sticks to whatever it strikes for a random time, then leaves at a random angle (Knudsen's cosine law) a little hotter than before. Its temperature is its speed, and its speed is your score.

Element shop

Elements unlock in order from light to heavy. Buy element Z for Z coins (you earn 1 coin per paddle hit, plus 1 coin per whole µK of your best temperature when a game ends), or win it for free by reaching its threshold temperature while playing element Z−1, or by ending a Sorting game that includes element Z−1 with a net entropy reduction of at least 2 + ⌈Z/3⌉ k_B. Sorting pays 1 coin per k_B of net entropy removed. Coins and unlocks are shared by all modes. Every element starts at the same on-screen speed, so heavier atoms start (and score) hotter.
