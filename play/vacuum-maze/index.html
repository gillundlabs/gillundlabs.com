Vacuum Maze

An apparatus for the extraction of single atoms from laboratory tubing, after Knudsen (1909)

One cold atom is loose in a maze of stainless-steel vacuum pipe. Tip the apparatus (your phone) and real gravity pulls it along. It doesn't bounce like a marble: it sticks to the steel for a moment, then leaves at a random angle (Knudsen's cosine law) with the wall's temperature. Steer it into a vacuum pump.
