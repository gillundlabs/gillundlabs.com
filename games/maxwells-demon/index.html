Maxwell's Demon

A Pong-like duel where the ball is a single atom.

Play in browser

App Store coming soonGoogle Play coming soonSteam coming soon

The browser version is free for the first three elements. The full game is coming to iPhone, Android and Steam.

A Pong-like game where the ball is a single atom, drawn as an antique Rutherford model. Everything is drawn procedurally; there are no image assets.

The atom obeys real wall physics: it sticks for a while, leaves at the wall's temperature, and heads off in a cosine-law direction. Heat atoms up to unlock heavier elements, mix gases, play a friend on one screen, or play the Sorting mode, where you work the trapdoor as Maxwell's demon and are scored on the entropy you remove.

Its in-game notebook, The Physics, is reproduced in full in our Notebook.
