Fantastic Apparatus

Build brass-and-steam contraptions on a Victorian lab bench.

Play in browser

App Store coming soonGoogle Play coming soonSteam coming soon

The browser version is free for the first three levels. The full game is coming to iPhone, Android and Steam.

A physics construction puzzle set in a Victorian laboratory, inspired by Fantastic Contraption. Each experiment has a goal object (a brass standard weight, a quartz sphere, a fire brick or a glass flask) and a brass specimen tray.

You build a contraption inside the blue build area out of brass wheels, steel rods, string and steam pistons, then pull the brass lever. The experiment succeeds when every goal object is wholly inside the tray for two seconds. Real masses, real torque, and Earth, Mars or Moon gravity.

Its in-game notebook, Laboratory notes, is reproduced in full in our Notebook.
